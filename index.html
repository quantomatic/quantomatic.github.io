Quantomatic

Simple guides to logic, patterns and math reasoning for middle and high school students.

Why reasoning skills matter

Many school entrance and placement tests check how well students spot patterns, follow logical steps and solve multi-step word problems. Practicing these skills a little each day builds speed and confidence.

Three habits that help

  • Write down what the question gives you and what it asks for.
  • Draw a quick diagram or table before calculating.
  • Check your answer by plugging it back into the problem.
